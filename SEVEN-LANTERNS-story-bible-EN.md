# SEVEN LANTERNS
### Story bible — 2D samurai platformer
**Rule: less text, more play. The story is shown, not told.**

**Logline:** A young ronin crosses the gates between worlds to find the friend who saved him — and learns that every gate keeps whoever wins.

**Total dialogue budget for the entire game: 24 lines.** Each line is 2–5 words. Everything else is visual.

---

## 1. DIALOGUE BUDGET (hard limit)

| World | Dialogue lines | How the rest is told |
|-------|----------------|----------------------|
| Prologue | 1 | running, fire, the hat handoff |
| 1 — Bamboo Forest | 3 | background details, a cold campfire |
| 2 — Fog Keep | 3 | four wordless wall carvings |
| 3 — Ash Plain | 3 | a burnt suit of armor |
| 4 — Mirror Lake | **0** | reflections only |
| 5 — Lantern Garden | 4 | Kaito's empty eyes |
| 6 — The Gate | 5 | the mark inside the helmet |
| Endings | 5 | the final shot |

If a line can be cut and the story still reads — **cut it**.

---

## 2. THE DEEP LAYER — what the game is really about

This is not a simple "find my friend" story. It is a story about a **loop**.

> A gate cannot stand without a keeper.
> Whoever defeats the keeper takes his place.
> The blue armor is a prison, not an enemy.

Ren is not the first. Dozens of travelers came before him, fought, won — and stayed inside the armor. **Aoshi's armor is empty because the man inside it has already been forgotten.**

And the core of it: **Kaito did this once for Ren.** In the prologue he pushes Ren through the gate and stays behind. The whole game is Ren repaying that debt.

**None of this is ever said out loud.** Three things carry it:

**① THE STRAW HAT** — In the prologue, Kaito takes off his own hat and puts it on Ren.
Throughout the game, **old, torn straw hats** hang in the background: on a branch, on a keep wall, under the ice, beneath a lantern. In World 1 the player doesn't notice. By World 4 they start counting. By World 6 they're uneasy.

**② THE ARMORS** — World 2 holds a hundred empty suits of armor. At the foot of each one lies a small object: a child's toy, a clay cup, a comb. Each one was a life.

**③ THE REFLECTION** — In World 4, the water shows Ren wearing the blue armor. One second. Never mentioned.

## 3. CHARACTERS (introduced without speaking)

**REN** — straw hat, pale kimono, scar on his cheek. He doesn't talk, he moves. In idle animation he straightens his hat. At low HP he holds onto it.

**KAITO** — black kimono, orange hat, prayer beads. Appears only in the prologue and World 5. Everywhere else he is a **shadow**: a silhouette crossing the background for one second. Players who chase it find a hidden bonus.

**HANA** — she never waits for Ren. She is **always one step ahead**. The player doesn't see her, only her traces:
- a campfire still warm
- a bandage tied to a branch
- food left on a rock (restores HP)
- small footprints in the snow

She appears on screen **twice** in the whole game: after World 2, and in Ending C. That's why both moments land.

**AOSHI** — never runs, never shouts, never looks at you. Even mid-fight he seems **bored**. Because he has done this a hundred times.

## 4. THE WORLDS — gameplay first, story inside it

### PROLOGUE — 20 seconds, fully playable
A burning village, side view. **Controls: run right. That's all.**
Ren falls behind → Kaito stops, **takes off his hat and puts it on Ren**, shoves him forward.
A golden gate. White out.

> **KAITO:** Run.

*(One line. Nothing else.)*

---

### WORLD 1 — BAMBOO FOREST · 4 stages
**Mechanics (taught, never explained):** double jump → wall cling → sword strike → parry.
Hotaru, a small white light, leads the way — **a glow instead of an arrow**.

**Visual storytelling:**
- Stage 2 background: an **old straw hat** hanging from a branch (first clue)
- Stage 3: a cold campfire and a bandage (Hana passed through)
- A black-kimono silhouette crosses the background for one second

**Boss — GONZA:** a bandit wearing a **fragment of blue armor** on his shoulder. Salvaged loot.
> **GONZA:** Leave the hat.
> **REN:** No.
> *(defeated, walking away)* **GONZA:** Don't go to the keep.

---

### WORLD 2 — FOG KEEP · 4 stages
**Mechanics:** moving platforms, slippery rain, chain swings.

**Visual storytelling — four carvings on the wall, no text:**
1. A traveler arrives at the gate
2. A fight with the armor
3. The traveler wins
4. The traveler puts the armor on

*(The player learns the whole rule from four pictures. Nobody explains it.)*

**Boss — AOSHI (fight 1, the player loses):**
Scripted, but honest about it: at 20% HP the music **stops**, the armor glows, one strike ends it.
> **AOSHI:** Too late.
> *(leaving, without turning around)* **AOSHI:** Go home.

**Waking up:** Ren by a campfire. **HANA is sitting there** (first appearance).
> **HANA:** I'm looking for someone too. You.

*(She stands and leaves. Ren doesn't follow. This is where it hurts.)*

---

### WORLD 3 — ASH PLAIN · 4 stages
**Mechanics:** falling ash drains HP — a breath timer. Lava, collapsing platforms, fast tempo.

**Visual storytelling:** a **burnt blue armor** driven into the ground. Beside it, the ashes of a straw hat.

**Boss — ENJU, the burning ronin:** a traveler who refused the armor. That's why he burned.
> **ENJU:** Don't wear it.
> **REN:** Why?
> *(fading)* **ENJU:** They told me the same.

**LANTERN 1.**

---

### WORLD 4 — MIRROR LAKE · 3 stages · **ZERO DIALOGUE**
**Mechanics:** the screen splits into the world above and its reflection. Some platforms exist **only in the reflection**. One button flips you between them.

**Boss — SHADOW REN:** copies the player's inputs with a one-second delay.
Mid-fight, for a single second: **the water shows Ren in the blue armor.** The music cuts out.
When Shadow Ren is beaten he doesn't fall — he **bows**, then scatters.

**LANTERN 2.** Nobody says anything. That's the power of this world.

---

### WORLD 5 — LANTERN GARDEN · 3 stages
**Mechanics:** almost no enemies. Quiet and slow. Light lanterns to open the path (small puzzles).
*(A breather. After four worlds the player needs one.)*

**The spirits don't speak** — they only watch and bow. One of them hands Ren a lantern.

**KAITO:** at the top of a staircase, back turned, a lantern in his hand.
> **REN:** Kaito.
> **KAITO:** *(turns — his eyes are empty)* Who?
> **REN:** Me.
> **KAITO:** *(a beat — light returns to his eyes)* ...Ren?

*(Aoshi takes him. → **CHASE SEQUENCE**: forced running, lanterns dying behind you, the screen going dark. No combat, just speed.)*

**LANTERN 3** — dropped by Kaito.

---

### WORLD 6 — THE GATE · FINALE

**AOSHI — three phases:**
1. **Heavy armor** — slow, powerful, ground shakes
2. **Cracked armor** — fast, fire seams, parry required
3. **Empty armor** — the helmet flies off and **there is no one inside**

**The key shot:** the helmet hits the ground, the camera pushes in — scratched on the inner plate is a **straw hat symbol**. Someone carved it to remember themselves.

> **AOSHI:** Beat me.
> **REN:** And then?
> **AOSHI:** Then you.
> *(the armor pulls toward Ren)*
> **AOSHI:** Thank you.

## 5. ENDINGS

### A — "THE PLACE" *(0–6 lanterns)*
Ren puts on the armor. Kaito wakes, Hana leads him through the gate.
**Final shot:** years later. A new traveler enters the forest. The blue armor waits at the gate. On its head — **a straw hat**.
*(The loop continues. Not a single word.)*

### B — "BREAK IT" *(refuse the armor at the finale)*
Ren shatters the armor. The gate collapses. Kaito comes back, but the worlds bleed into each other.
**Final shot:** the village rebuilding, two moons in the sky.
> **KAITO:** We broke something.
> **REN:** Yes.

### C — "SEVEN LANTERNS" *(7/7 lanterns — true ending)*
Ren puts on the armor. The gate is closing.
**Hana comes back through it.** She walks up to the armored Ren and **places the hat on his head**.
The armor cracks apart — the curse can erase a name, but not **being remembered**.
The old traveler inside Aoshi is freed and closes the gate **himself**.
**Final shot:** the three of them on the road. Ren without his hat. Hana wearing it.
> **AOSHI:** *(fading)* No one ever waited for me.

*(Ending C is 5 lines. The strongest ending has the fewest words.)*

## 6. WORDLESS STORYTELLING TOOLKIT

Scatter these through the levels — they do the work text would have done:

| Device | Where |
|--------|-------|
| Recurring background object (the hat) | 1–2 per world |
| A personal item at the foot of each armor | World 2 |
| Cold campfire, bandage, food (Hana's trail) | 1 per world |
| Wordless wall carvings | Worlds 2 and 6 |
| Silhouette crossing the background (Kaito) | Worlds 1, 3, 5 |
| Boss body language (a bored Aoshi) | every fight |
| Reflections | World 4 |
| The music cutting out | 3 key moments |
| Color: the hat fades from straw-gold to grey | across the game |

---

## 7. PACING (so the player never gets bored)

```
Prologue 20s → Forest 6min → Keep 7min → Ash 7min
→ Mirror 5min → Lanterns 4min (quiet) → Finale 6min
```
**About 35 minutes total.** Ideal for CrazyGames: finishable in one sitting, replayable for the seven lanterns.

Something **new every 90 seconds**: a new enemy, a new mechanic, or a background shift.
Dialogue **never** stops the game — Ren keeps walking, the bubble floats above him.

---

## 8. WHERE TO START

1. **Prologue + World 1 + Gonza** — the vertical slice. Prove the story works on four lines of dialogue here.
2. Place the **first straw hat** in the background. If a playtester remembers it by World 4, the storytelling works.
3. **The Aoshi fight (loss)** — this is the hook, and the trailer moment.
4. Then the remaining worlds.

Keep Uzbek as a secondary language. Twenty-four short lines are trivial to localize — that is one more reason to keep the text count low.
